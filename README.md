# About Me

I am a PhD student at Queen Mary University of London in the Applied and Stochastic Topology group; supervised by Prof. Omer Bobrowski and Prof. Primoz Skraba. My research primarily concerns deep learning models on spatial point processes. Before starting my PhD, I worked as a Data Scientist at PwC.
